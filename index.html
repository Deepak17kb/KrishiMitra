      Central schemes

      Schemes for farmers

      • PM-KISAN ₹6,000 a year in three instalments of ₹2,000, paid straight to the bank account of eligible landholding farmer families. e-KYC is required.
        pmkisan.gov.in
      • PM Fasal Bima Yojana Crop insurance against weather, pest and disease loss. The farmer pays 2% of the sum insured for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi and 5% for commercial and horticultural crops.
        pmfby.gov.in
      • Kisan Credit Card Short-term crop loans at 7% interest. Repay on time and the effective rate comes down to 4%. Apply at your bank or cooperative society.
        myscheme.gov.in
      • Soil Health Card Free soil testing, with fertilizer recommendations for the crops you grow on that field.
        soilhealth.dac.gov.in
      • Per Drop More Crop (PMKSY) Subsidy on drip and sprinkler systems — usually 55% for small and marginal farmers and 45% for others.
        pmksy.gov.in
      • Farm machinery subsidy (SMAM) Help with the cost of tractors, implements and harvest machinery — usually 40–50% depending on the farmer category.
        agrimachinery.nic.in
      • e-NAM Sell produce online to buyers across mandis, and compare prices before you sell.
        enam.gov.in

      Amounts and rules change, and states add their own conditions. The official site has the final word.

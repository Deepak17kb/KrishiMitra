      Government help

      Schemes you can use and numbers you can call.

      🏛️

      Schemes for you

      • PM-KISAN ₹6,000 every year, sent to your bank account in 3 parts.
        Open site
      • PM Fasal Bima Crop insurance. You pay only 2% for Kharif crops and 1.5% for Rabi crops.
        Open site
      • Kisan Credit Card Low-cost crop loan. Only 4% interest if you repay on time.
        Open site
      • Soil Health Card Free soil test. It tells you how much fertilizer your field needs.
        Open site
      • Drip and sprinkler subsidy The government pays about half the cost of drip and sprinkler sets.
        Open site
      • Farm machine subsidy 40 to 50% help on the cost of tractors and farm machines.
        Open site
      • e-NAM (online mandi) Sell your crop online and see prices in other mandis.
        Open site

      Rules can change. Check the official site.
